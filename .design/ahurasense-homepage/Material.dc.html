Art direction — material Shot brief · v1

The photograph is the design. So the photograph has to be yours.

Four compositions below. Each one is built so that type sits inside the image rather than beside it — no container, no border, no gradient. That only works if the image carries real tonal depth, which no asset currently in public/ does.

So the grey blocks are honest placeholders, not design. Each carries the shot it needs: subject, crop, focal length, exposure, and which region of the frame has to stay dark enough to hold text. Hand it to whoever shoots, or use it to brief a licensing search.

01 — Hero, full bleed 1300 × 620
Image 01
Sovereign compute, for India
Your AI stack.
Your jurisdiction.
Inference, GPUs, Kubernetes and managed data — operated end to end inside Indian borders, billed by the second in rupees.
100%
Data resident in India
Subject. Rack aisle, shot long down the cold aisle — or a single populated rack if that is all you have access to.

Crop & lens. 24–35mm, camera low, aisle vanishing to the right third. Leave the lower-left quadrant free of detail.

Exposure. Two to three stops under. Let the blacks crush. Only status LEDs and the aisle floor catch light.

Must hold type. Lower-left 620×280 and lower-right 200×90 stay under 12% luminance.
02 — Residency, macro diptych Two frames, unequal
Image 02a
Where your data sits is an architecture decision.
Subject. Macro of a single accelerator board — heatsink fins, VRM, the NVLink edge connector. Shallow depth.

Lens. 90–105mm macro, f/4. Board at a slight angle, not flat-on.

Light. One hard raking source from the left. No fill. The fins should read as ridges of light against black.
Image 02b
No cross-border failover. No foreign subprocessor holding your keys. Replication targets validated against an in-country allowlist — enforced in the API, not left to operator discipline.
Subject. Cabling — a bundle of fibre or DAC entering a switch, tight and orderly.

Why. It reads as craft. Tidy cabling is the visual shorthand every infrastructure buyer already trusts.

Tone. Match 02a. Same session, same light.

04 — Close Half bleed
Built for institutions that answer to a regulator, not a status page.

A global provider's region is a location. Its jurisdiction follows its parent company. Ours does not — and for a regulated Indian workload that distinction is the entire procurement conversation.

Request an architecture review Read the residency model
Image 04
Subject. A person. One engineer at a crash cart in an aisle, or hands seating a drive — shot from behind or in profile, never posed to camera.

Why it closes the page. Three frames of hardware establish capability. The fourth has to establish that people operate it. Every enterprise site does this, and it is the frame most often skipped.

Lens. 50mm, f/2, available light only.
